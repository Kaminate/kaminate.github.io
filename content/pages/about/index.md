Hi, My name is Nate Park. I'm an origami artist and video game programmer. My origami artwork has been featured in the OrigamiUSA NYC Convention. Professionaly, I specialize in gameplay and graphics programming. I have more than 10 years experience in the game industry, and have contributed to various titles, such as Call of Duty (Treyarch/Sledgehammer Games), Forza Motorsport (Microsoft/Turn 10), The Last of Us (Sony/Naughty Dog), VR (Ready at Dawn/Meta), and also architecture software (Vectorworks).
